About Me

THE EDITOR BEHIND THE CUT

I'm Muhanad Wael Mahdy, a video editor and motion designer with 2+ years of experience turning raw footage into stories people finish, share and remember. My work spans short-form virality, long-form documentaries, commercials and esports montages — always with the same obsession: rhythm, emotion and polish in every frame.

Great editing is invisible. I start every project by understanding the story and the audience, then let rhythm and pacing do the heavy lifting. Whether it's a 15-second Reel or a 12-minute documentary, the goal is the same: make people feel something before they realize they're watching an edit.
